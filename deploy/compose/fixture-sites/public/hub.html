Admission to B.Sc. studies for foreign candidates

Candidates holding a foreign secondary school certificate must have it recognised in Poland before the start of studies. The certificate must allow the holder to apply for higher education in the country where it was issued, and it must be legalised or carry an apostille.

Applications are submitted through the online registration system. After registration, candidates pay the application fee and upload scans of the required documents before the deadline published for the current recruitment round.
